GOOD QUESTIONS

Curious?
Us too.

We're still building the foundations. Here's what we can tell you so far.

Can I book a Phomer Provider today? +

Not yet. Phomer is a pre-launch business. We're validating demand and planning the first version of the platform before taking live bookings.

Where is Phomer launching? +

We're planning to start in Greater Glasgow, refine the service using real feedback and expand step by step.

What kind of jobs will Phomer cover? +

Our vision spans everyday local services such as help around the house, cleaning, gardening, furniture assembly and other practical jobs. Exact services and provider requirements will be confirmed during development.

Who are Phomer Providers? +

Phomer Providers are local service providers, tradespeople and skilled individuals who can offer their services through Phomer. We're designing the platform to help them find work that fits their skills and availability.

How will providers be checked? +

Identity checks, profiles, reviews and relevant verification processes are part of our planned marketplace design. The specific standards will be confirmed before launch.
