What is Phomer?

Think Golden Pages meets Deliveroo!

Phomer is a planned live app that connects people needing local services with nearby providers who are available to work. No endless searching, chasing replies or waiting for someone to call you back.

Open the app→ Find your service→ Connect and discuss the job→ Book instantly

Help could arrive quicker than a pizza! Or you could book for a later time that suits you both. And for providers, it's a way to turn free time into paid work by switching yourself “On Phomer” whenever you're available.

That's the vision we're testing. Phomer is pre-launch, and instant availability will depend on location, service and provider acceptance.
